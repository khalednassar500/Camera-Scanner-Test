Tips for a fair test

  • Use the back camera. Fill most of the box with the barcode and hold it still.
  • If it is blurry, move it a little farther away — sharp beats big.
  • Try shiny, curved and coloured packs, and in your shop's real light.
  • If a product never reads, tap Save frame while it is in the box and send me the image.
